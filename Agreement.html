Web Design & Development Service Agreement

Service Agreement Overview

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Remlyx ("Company", "we", "us") and the client ("Client", "you") for web design and development services. This agreement outlines the terms, scope, and expectations for our professional services.

Scope of Services

Remlyx will provide the following services as agreed upon in the project proposal:

  • Website design and user interface development;
  • Frontend and backend development;
  • Content management system integration;
  • Responsive design for mobile and tablet devices;
  • Basic search engine optimization (SEO);
  • Quality assurance testing across multiple browsers and devices;
  • Training on content management (if applicable);
  • 30 days of post-launch support for bug fixes.

Project Timeline

The estimated project timeline will be provided in the individual project proposal. Timelines are dependent on:

  • Prompt provision of required content, images, and materials by the client;
  • Timely feedback and approval of design concepts and revisions;
  • Complexity of requested features and functionality;
  • Third-party integrations and external dependencies.

Delays in client responses or content provision may extend the project timeline accordingly.

Client Responsibilities

To ensure successful project completion, the client agrees to:

  • Provide all necessary content, images, and materials in a timely manner;
  • Participate in regular project reviews and provide constructive feedback;
  • Make payments according to the agreed schedule;
  • Provide access to hosting accounts, domain registrars, and third-party services as needed;
  • Review and approve design concepts and development milestones promptly;
  • Communicate any changes or concerns in a timely manner.

Payment Terms

Our standard payment structure is as follows:

  • 50% deposit required before project commencement;
  • 25% payment due at design approval stage;
  • 25% final payment due upon project completion and delivery;
  • Payments are due within 14 days of invoice date;
  • Late payments may incur a 5% monthly service charge;
  • All prices are quoted in GBP and exclude VAT unless stated otherwise.

Revisions and Additional Work

The project proposal includes a specified number of revision rounds:

  • Design phase: Up to 3 rounds of revisions included;
  • Development phase: Up to 2 rounds of minor revisions included;
  • Additional revisions beyond the agreed scope will be charged at our standard hourly rate;
  • Major scope changes require written approval and may affect timeline and cost;
  • Rush requests may incur additional charges.

Intellectual Property and Ownership

Upon full payment of all fees:

  • Client receives full ownership of custom design and code created specifically for their project;
  • Client receives usage rights for any licensed stock photography or graphics purchased for the project;
  • Remlyx retains rights to any pre-existing code libraries, frameworks, or third-party components;
  • Remlyx reserves the right to showcase the completed work in our portfolio and marketing materials;
  • Client is responsible for obtaining proper licenses for any third-party software or plugins.

Hosting and Maintenance

Website hosting and ongoing maintenance are separate services:

  • Client is responsible for securing and paying for web hosting;
  • We can recommend hosting providers and assist with setup;
  • Ongoing maintenance plans are available separately;
  • Post-launch support is included for 30 days for bug fixes only;
  • Content updates and feature additions require separate agreements.

Launch and Go-Live Process

The website launch process includes:

  • Thorough testing across multiple browsers and devices;
  • Final client review and approval;
  • DNS configuration and hosting setup assistance;
  • SSL certificate installation (if applicable);
  • Basic analytics setup and configuration;
  • Handover of login credentials and documentation.

Limitations and Disclaimers

Please note the following limitations:

  • We cannot guarantee specific search engine rankings or traffic levels;
  • Cross-browser compatibility is ensured for current versions of major browsers;
  • Mobile responsiveness is optimized for standard screen sizes;
  • Third-party integration functionality depends on external service availability;
  • Content accuracy and legal compliance are the client's responsibility.

Cancellation and Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement with written notice:

  • Client cancellation: Payment is due for all work completed to date;
  • Work-in-progress will be delivered in its current state;
  • Deposits are non-refundable after work has commenced;
  • Upon termination, any work product will be delivered as-is;
  • Outstanding invoices remain due regardless of project status.

Support and Warranty

We provide the following support and warranty:

  • 30 days of complimentary bug fix support post-launch;
  • Technical support for issues directly related to our development work;
  • Warranty does not cover hosting issues, third-party service problems, or content changes;
  • Extended support and maintenance plans available separately;
  • Training documentation provided for content management systems.
