    Remlyx Privacy Policy

    This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you visit, subscribe, register or make a purchase from https://remlyx.com, ‘Site’.

    Log Files

    Our website automatically collects certain information about your device, including information about your web browser, IP address, time zone, and some of the cookies that are installed on your device. Additionally, as you browse the site, we collect information about the individual web pages or products that you view, what websites or search terms referred you to the site, and information about how you interact with the site.

    Personal Information

    We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on our site, place an order, subscribe to our newsletter, respond to a survey, or enter information on our site. This may include your name, email address, phone number, and other contact information. We use this information to provide you with the services you request and to communicate with you about our services.

    We collect Device Information using the following technologies:

    • Cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance user experience and site functionality;
    • Log files that record information about your browser, device, and how you interact with our services;
    • Web beacons and pixel tags to understand how you navigate through our website;
    • Device information including IP address, browser type, operating system, and mobile device identifiers;
    • Usage data such as pages visited, time spent on pages, and interactions with our services.

    We use various technologies to collect and store information about your device and how you interact with our services. This helps us improve our website functionality and provide you with a better user experience. All data collection is performed in accordance with applicable privacy laws and regulations.

    Telemetry Data

    We collect and process telemetry data to improve our services and user experience. This includes usage statistics, performance metrics, and error reports that help us identify and resolve issues. All data collection is done in compliance with privacy regulations and user consent.

    We collect and process your personal data to provide our services effectively. This includes information you provide directly to us, such as contact details, and data we collect automatically through your use of our services. We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information.

    Our data processing practices are designed to protect your privacy while enabling us to deliver high-quality services. We use industry-standard security measures and regularly update our privacy practices to comply with evolving regulations.

    We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. Our privacy practices comply with applicable data protection laws and regulations.
